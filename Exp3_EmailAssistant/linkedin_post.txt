📧🤖 Built an Intelligent Email Assistant that drafts customer-support replies!

In my 24CS2506 – Building Applications using GPT-4 lab, I built an email assistant that learns from 100 past customer conversations and runs locally with Ollama.

How it works:
1️⃣ Detects the customer's intent (delivery issue, refund, payment issue, complaint …)
2️⃣ Retrieves the most similar past email using sentence embeddings
3️⃣ Generates a professional, context-aware reply with Llama 3.2
4️⃣ Rewrites the reply in a Friendly, Formal or Empathetic tone
5️⃣ Summarizes the conversation in ~50 words and saves the reply with intent and date

Tech: Python • Ollama • Llama 3.2 • Sentence-Transformers • Pandas
