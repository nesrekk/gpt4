⚖️🤖 Built an Automated Legal Document Analyzer with a local LLM!

For my 24CS2506 – Building Applications using GPT-4 lab at Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences, I built a tool that reads contracts and turns them into a clear, structured report, running fully offline with Ollama.

What it does:
✅ Reads contracts from PDF, Word or text files, or pasted text
✅ Cleans the text and finds dates, amounts and notice periods with rule-based checks
✅ Writes a plain-English summary and identifies the parties
✅ Extracts key clauses, each party's obligations, payment terms, termination conditions and important dates
✅ Lets you choose Summary, Clause, Obligation or Complete analysis, and saves the report

Tech: Python • Ollama (Llama 3.2, JSON mode) • PyMuPDF • python-docx • Prompt Engineering

Note: this is an educational project, not legal advice.
