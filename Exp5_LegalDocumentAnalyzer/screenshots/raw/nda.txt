SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------
BrightPath Technologies Private Limited and Nila Analytics LLP have entered into a mutual non-
disclosure agreement to explore a possible collaboration on an AI-based demand forecasting
platform for retail clients. The agreement requires the parties to keep confidential certain
business, technical, and financial information. The receiving party must use the confidential
information only for the purpose, protect it with reasonable care, and return it upon request.
The agreement will remain in force for two years and can be terminated by either party with 30
days' prior written notice. If a party breaches the agreement, the other party may seek
injunctive relief and liquidated damages of INR 5,00,000 in case of proven unauthorized
disclosure.

KEY CLAUSES
------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
   The Parties agree to explore a possible collaboration to build an AI-based demand forecasting
   platform for retail clients.
2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
   Confidential Information includes any business, technical or financial information disclosed
   by one Party to the other Party, including source code, datasets, customer lists, pricing,
   product roadmaps and trade secrets.
4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY
   The Receiving Party must use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose, not disclose
   it without prior written consent, and protect it with reasonable care.
5. RETURN OF INFORMATION
   The Receiving Party must return or destroy all Confidential Information within 15 days of a
   written request or on termination of the Agreement.
6. TERM AND TERMINATION
   This Agreement remains in force for two years from the Effective Date and can be terminated
   by either Party with 30 days' prior written notice.
8. REMEDIES
   The breaching Party must pay liquidated damages of INR 5,00,000 for each proven unauthorised
   disclosure.
9. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
   This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts at Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu have
   exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute.
10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
   This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and may be
   amended only in writing signed by both Parties.
