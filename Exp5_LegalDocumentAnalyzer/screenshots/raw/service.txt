SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------
This agreement outlines the terms for a software development project between GreenLeaf Retail
and CodeCraft Solutions. The Service Provider will design, develop, test, and deploy a mobile
shopping application for the Client. The project timeline includes milestones and payment terms.
The Service Provider is responsible for delivering the services with reasonable skill and care,
and the Client must provide product data and make payments on time. The agreement also includes
provisions for intellectual property, termination, limitation of liability, and dispute
resolution.

KEY CLAUSES
------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
   The Service Provider is responsible for designing, developing, testing, and deploying an
   Android and iOS mobile shopping application for the Client.
3. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
   The total fee for the Services is INR 6,00,000, payable in three installments: 30% as
   advance, 30% on acceptance of Milestone 2, and 40% within 15 days of the final release.
4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE SERVICE PROVIDER
   The Service Provider must perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, assign
   qualified developers, fix bugs within 90 days, and keep the Client's data confidential.
5. OBLIGATIONS OF THE CLIENT
   The Client must provide product data, logos, and content within 5 working days, give feedback
   on deliverables within 7 days, and make payments on time.
6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   On receipt of full payment, all rights in the source code and designs created under this
   Agreement transfer to the Client.
8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
   The Service Provider's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees
   paid by the Client.
9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
   Any dispute shall first be resolved through good-faith negotiation for 30 days, failing which
   it shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator in Coimbatore under the Arbitration
   and Conciliation Act, 1996.
7. TERMINATION
   Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice, with the Client
   paying for all work completed up to the termination date and the Service Provider handing
   over all work in progress.

PAYMENT TERMS
------------------------------------------------------------
 • 30% (INR 1,80,000) as advance within 7 days of signing this Agreement
 • 30% (INR 1,80,000) on acceptance of Milestone 2
 • 40% (INR 2,40,000) within 15 days of the final release

TERMINATION CONDITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
 • Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice
 • The Client may terminate immediately if the Service Provider materially breaches this
   Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 15 days of written notice
