MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on 1 August 2026 ("Effective Date")
by and between:

BrightPath Technologies Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013,
having its registered office at 14 Race Course Road, Coimbatore 641018, Tamil Nadu ("BrightPath"); and

Nila Analytics LLP, a limited liability partnership having its office at 22 Anna Salai,
Chennai 600002, Tamil Nadu ("Nila Analytics").

BrightPath and Nila Analytics are each referred to as a "Party" and together as the "Parties".

1. PURPOSE
The Parties wish to explore a possible collaboration to build an AI-based demand forecasting
platform for retail clients (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may
disclose Confidential Information to the other Party.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means any business, technical or financial information disclosed by
one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), including source
code, datasets, customer lists, pricing, product roadmaps and trade secrets, whether disclosed
orally, in writing or electronically, and whether or not marked as confidential.

3. EXCLUSIONS
Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes publicly available
without breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure;
(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information;
or (d) is received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY
The Receiving Party shall:
(a) use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
(b) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent
    of the Disclosing Party, except to its employees and advisers who need to know it for the
    Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as this Agreement;
(c) protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own
    confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care;
(d) promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing, and in any case within 48 hours, of any
    unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

5. RETURN OF INFORMATION
Within 15 days of a written request by the Disclosing Party, or on termination of this Agreement,
the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify the same in
writing.

6. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain in force for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date.
Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the
other Party. The confidentiality obligations in Clause 4 shall survive for three (3) years after
the expiry or termination of this Agreement.

7. NO LICENCE
Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any licence or right in the other Party's
intellectual property, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

8. REMEDIES
The Parties agree that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm, and the Disclosing
Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available
under law. The breaching Party shall pay liquidated damages of INR 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh) for
each proven unauthorised disclosure.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. The courts at Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu shall
have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and may be amended
only in writing signed by both Parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have signed this Agreement on the Effective Date.
